How we do it

A van and one or two of us is the right size for most flat moves and smaller houses: a studio, a one or two-bedroom flat, a small house where the furniture fits in one or two trips. You get the lifting and carrying without paying for a removal lorry and a crew of four.

Send us both addresses, the date and a list or a few photos of what is going. We tell you how long it should take and what it costs before you book.

What we move

  • Beds, wardrobes and chests of drawers
  • Sofas, tables and chairs
  • Washing machines and fridges
  • Boxes, bags and suitcases
  • Bikes, garden furniture and plants

Good to know

Stairs and lifts

Tell us the floor at both ends and whether there is a lift. It changes how long the move takes, not whether we can do it.

Clear out as you go

Things you are not taking can leave in the same visit. We are a licensed waste carrier, so they are disposed of properly.

How the price works

We price a move by how much is going, the distance and the carrying at each end: stairs, lifts, parking. You get a free estimate before you book.

Common Questions

How do you price a move?ShowHide

By how much is moving, the distance between the two addresses and how much carrying there is: stairs, lifts, a long walk from where the van can park. Send us both postcodes, the date and a list or a few photos, and we give you a price before you book.

Do you do the carrying?ShowHide

Yes. We carry everything out, load and secure it in the van, and carry it into the right rooms at the other end. Bigger moves get two of us; on a small move you can help load and pay less.

Can you take apart beds and wardrobes?ShowHide

Yes, flat-pack beds, wardrobes and tables. Mention it when you send the list, so we bring the right tools and allow the time.

Can you take away what I am not moving?ShowHide

Yes, and it is where we differ from most man and van firms. We are registered with the Environment Agency as a waste carrier, so old furniture, mattresses and bags of rubbish can go to licensed sites in the same trip, legally.

Can you collect from a shop or a Marketplace seller?ShowHide

Yes. Send us the collection address, the delivery address and a photo or link of the item, and we tell you the price and when we can do it.
