Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what most people ask before they book.

View more FAQs
How do you price a move?

By how much is moving, the distance between the two addresses and how much carrying there is: stairs, lifts, a long walk from where the van can park. Send us both postcodes, the date and a list or a few photos, and we give you a price before you book.

Do you do the carrying?

Yes. We carry everything out, load and secure it in the van, and carry it into the right rooms at the other end. Bigger moves get two of us; on a small move you can help load and pay less.

Can you take apart beds and wardrobes?

Yes, flat-pack beds, wardrobes and tables. Mention it when you send the list, so we bring the right tools and allow the time.

Can you take away what I am not moving?

Yes, and it is where we differ from most man and van firms. We are registered with the Environment Agency as a waste carrier, so old furniture, mattresses and bags of rubbish can go to licensed sites in the same trip, legally.

Can you collect from a shop or a Marketplace seller?

Yes. Send us the collection address, the delivery address and a photo or link of the item, and we tell you the price and when we can do it.
