How much does rubbish removal cost in Bournemouth?

We do not use a fixed price list, because two loads that look alike in a photo can be very different jobs. Here is what the price depends on, and how to get a free estimate quote in a few minutes.

What the price depends on

  • 1

    How much of the van it fills

    A single sofa, a few bags, a quarter, half or a full van load. This is the biggest part of the price.

  • 2

    What the waste is

    Soil, rubble and turf are heavy, so the van fills by weight before it fills by space. Fridges and freezers are priced separately.

  • 3

    How easy it is to reach

    Kerbside or a ground-floor garage is quicker than a top-floor flat with no lift, a loft or the far end of a long garden.

  • 4

    Whether you help

    On small jobs one of us comes. Help load and you do not pay for a second person.

What is included

  • Carrying everything out and loading the van
  • Transport to licensed transfer and recycling sites
  • The disposal fees at those sites
  • Sweeping up where the waste was
  • A waste transfer note for businesses

Cheaper than a skip?

For most household jobs, yes. You pay for the waste we take, not for a skip that sits half empty on the drive. There is no skip permit to arrange, and we do the loading.

A skip can make sense for a long building job where waste comes out every day. For a clear-out, a garden or a few bulky items, a van collection is usually quicker and costs less.

Man and van prices

A move is priced by how much is going, the distance between the two addresses and the carrying at each end. Bigger moves get two of us; on a small move you can help load and pay less.

Send both postcodes, the date and a list or a few photos. See our man and van services or ask for a price on WhatsApp.

Questions about prices

Is the estimate free?ShowHide

Yes. Send a few photos on WhatsApp and we reply with a free estimate quote. There is no charge for the estimate and no obligation to book.

Can the price change on the day?ShowHide

From photos we can only estimate. We confirm the price when we see the waste, before anything goes in the van. If there is more or less than the photos showed, we tell you first and you decide.

What is included in the price?ShowHide

The loading, the transport, the disposal fees at licensed sites and sweeping up afterwards. Businesses also get a waste transfer note.

Is it cheaper if I help load?ShowHide

On small jobs, yes. One of us comes, and if you help load you do not pay for a second person. Tell us when you send the photos.

Do fridges and freezers cost more?ShowHide

Fridges and freezers go to a site that removes the gases safely, so they are priced separately from general waste.

How much does a man and van move cost?ShowHide

It depends on how much is moving, the distance between the two addresses and the carrying at each end: stairs, lifts, parking. Send both postcodes, the date and a list or a few photos, and we give you a free estimate quote before you book.
