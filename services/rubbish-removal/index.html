What the job involves

Most jobs start with a pile that has been growing for a while: bags by the side of the house, boxes in the hallway, an old chair nobody wants to drag to the tip. Send us a photo and we will tell you what it costs to make it disappear.

We carry everything out ourselves, from the back garden, upstairs flats or the loft, so you do not need to move anything to the kerb first. On small jobs, helping us load brings the price down.

Common Questions

How much does it cost?ShowHide

It depends on how much of the van your waste fills, what it is (rubble and soil are heavy, a sofa is bulky) and how easy it is to reach. Send us a few photos on WhatsApp and we reply with a free estimate. From photos we can only estimate, so we confirm the price when we see the waste, before we load. It includes the loading, the transport and the disposal fees.

How quickly can you come?ShowHide

Often the same day, seven days a week between 9am and 6pm. If today is full, we will give you the next free slot when we reply to your photos.

Do I have to carry anything outside?ShowHide

No, we carry everything out ourselves: from upstairs flats, lofts, garages, basements and the far end of the garden. On small jobs one of us comes, and many customers choose to help load to save the cost of a second person. Tell us what you prefer when you send the photos.

Are you licensed?ShowHide

Yes. We are a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, and we take what we collect to licensed sites. It matters: if an unlicensed collector fly-tips your rubbish, the fine can come to you.

Is it cheaper than a skip?ShowHide

For most household jobs, yes. You pay for the waste we take, not for a skip that sits half empty on the drive. There is no council permit to arrange, and we do the loading.
