People always ask

Rubbish and clearance

Short answers to what most people ask before they book.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much of the van your waste fills, what it is (rubble and soil are heavy, a sofa is bulky) and how easy it is to reach. Send us a few photos on WhatsApp and we reply with a fixed price. That price covers the loading, the transport and the disposal fees.

How quickly can you come?

Often the same day, seven days a week between 9am and 6pm. If today is full, we will give you the next free slot when we reply to your photos.

Do I have to carry anything outside?

No. There are two of us, and we carry everything out ourselves: from upstairs flats, lofts, garages, basements and the far end of the garden.

Are you licensed?

Yes. We are a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, and we take what we collect to licensed sites. It matters: if an unlicensed collector fly-tips your rubbish, the fine can come to you.

Is it cheaper than a skip?

For most household jobs, yes. You pay for the waste we take, not for a skip that sits half empty on the drive. There is no council permit to arrange, and we do the loading.

What can’t you take?

Hazardous waste such as asbestos, gas bottles, liquid paint, oils and chemicals has to go through specialist routes. If you are not sure about something, include it in your photos and we will tell you.

Can you take fridges and freezers?

Yes. Fridges and freezers go to a site that removes the gases safely, so they are priced separately from general waste.

Do I need to be at home?

Not always. If the waste is outside or someone can let us in, we can agree everything from your photos and send you pictures when the job is done.

Do you work for landlords, agents and solicitors?

Yes, regularly. We clear rentals between tenants and probate properties, and we can collect keys from an agent’s office.

Do I get a waste transfer note?

Businesses get one with every collection. If you are a householder and would like one for your records, just ask.

People always ask

Man and van moves

Short answers to what most people ask before they book.

Do you do moves as well as rubbish removal?

Yes. We do man and van moves for flats, small houses, students and offices, and we deliver single items of furniture. Because we are also a licensed waste carrier, anything you are not taking can leave in the same visit.

How do you price a move?

By how much is moving, the distance between the two addresses and how much carrying there is: stairs, lifts, a long walk from where the van can park. Send us both postcodes, the date and a list or a few photos, and we give you a price before you book.

Do you do the carrying?

Yes. There are two of us on every move. We carry everything out, load and secure it in the van, and carry it into the right rooms at the other end.

Can you take apart beds and wardrobes?

Yes, flat-pack beds, wardrobes and tables. Mention it when you send the list, so we bring the right tools and allow the time.

Can you take away what I am not moving?

Yes, and it is where we differ from most man and van firms. We are registered with the Environment Agency as a waste carrier, so old furniture, mattresses and bags of rubbish can go to licensed sites in the same trip, legally.

Can you collect from a shop or a Marketplace seller?

Yes. Send us the collection address, the delivery address and a photo or link of the item, and we tell you the price and when we can do it.

Need a price or got a question?

Send a few photos on WhatsApp and we reply with a fixed price. Or call us, seven days a week.
