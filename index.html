Waste Types We Remove In Your Area

What waste do we remove?

  • Household waste: Bags, boxes, furniture, appliances and general clutter, from single items to full loads.
  • Garden waste: Branches, hedge cuttings, leaves, grass, soil, old sheds and fencing.
  • Furniture and mattresses: Sofas, beds, wardrobes, tables, chairs and mattresses.
  • Fridges and freezers: Collected and taken to a site that removes the gases safely.
  • Rubble and hardcore: Bricks, concrete, tiles and soil from building work.
  • Builders’ waste: Plasterboard, timber, old bathroom suites and renovation waste.
  • Wood and metal: Old pallets, timber, scrap metal and garden furniture.
  • House and garage clearances: Whole properties, garages, lofts and sheds cleared in one visit.
  • Commercial waste: Offices, shops and restaurants, with a waste transfer note.

We cannot take asbestos, gas bottles, liquid paint, oils or chemicals. If you are not sure about something, put it in your photos and we will tell you.
