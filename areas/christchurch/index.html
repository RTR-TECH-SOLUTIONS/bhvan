Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what most people ask before they book.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much of the van your waste fills, what it is (rubble and soil are heavy, a sofa is bulky) and how easy it is to reach. Send us a few photos on WhatsApp and we reply with a fixed price. That price covers the loading, the transport and the disposal fees.

How quickly can you come?

Often the same day, seven days a week between 9am and 6pm. If today is full, we will give you the next free slot when we reply to your photos.

Do I have to carry anything outside?

No. There are two of us, and we carry everything out ourselves: from upstairs flats, lofts, garages, basements and the far end of the garden.

Are you licensed?

Yes. We are a registered waste carrier with the Environment Agency, and we take what we collect to licensed sites. It matters: if an unlicensed collector fly-tips your rubbish, the fine can come to you.

Is it cheaper than a skip?

For most household jobs, yes. You pay for the waste we take, not for a skip that sits half empty on the drive. There is no council permit to arrange, and we do the loading.
