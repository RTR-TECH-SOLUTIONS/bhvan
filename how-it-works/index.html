Licensed, and why that matters to you

We are a registered waste carrier, licensed by the Environment Agency. Everything we collect goes to licensed transfer and recycling sites, and we separate what can be reused or recycled.

It is worth checking with anyone you hire. By law, householders have a duty of care for their waste: if an unlicensed collector dumps it by the roadside, the fine can come back to you. Ask us for our licence details and we will send them with your quote.

Why not just hire a skip?

A skip makes sense for a long renovation. For most clear-outs it is the slower option, and often the dearer one.
